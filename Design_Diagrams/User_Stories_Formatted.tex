\textbf{Project:} In Home Assistive Robot / remote-care-bot

Our robot is a compact two-wheel robot with a front-facing screen. It lets a family caregiver have a video conversation with an older or disabled adult at home, and it can follow the resident from room to room so the conversation doesn't have to end when they get up. The resident decides when calls start, when the robot follows, and when it stops.

\section{Use cases}

\subsection*{UC-01 - Continue a caregiver call into another room}

\textbf{Linked story:} US-01 (also covers US-02 and US-04). \textbf{Primary actor:} Resident. \textbf{Secondary actors:} Remote caregiver; other people in the home.

\textbf{Preconditions:}

\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}

\item Caregiver C has the resident's permission to call, and FOLLOW is turned off.

\item The robot is parked next to the resident on a cleared single-floor route.

\item Both devices are online and send a heartbeat once per second.

\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Main success flow:}

\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}

\item \textbf{Caregiver:} Calls the resident.

\item \textbf{System:} Announces the call with C's name. The camera and microphone stay off.

\item \textbf{Resident:} Accepts the call.

\item \textbf{System:} Starts the call and shows that the camera and microphone are on.

\item \textbf{Resident:} Asks the robot to follow and walks toward another room.

\item \textbf{System:} Follows the resident at no more than 0.2 m/s and keeps the call going.

\item \textbf{Resident:} Stops in the new room.

\item \textbf{System:} Stops within 1 m of the resident and keeps the call going.

\item \textbf{Resident:} Ends the call.

\item \textbf{System:} Turns off the camera and microphone, turns FOLLOW off, and shows Ended.

\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Alternate flow A1 - Resident declines:} At step 3, the resident declines. The system tells C the call was declined, and the camera and microphone never turn on.

\textbf{Exception flow X1 - The robot loses the resident:} During step 6, the resident leaves the camera's view or another person walks between them. The robot stops and stays stopped, the call continues from where it stopped, and the screen asks the resident to restart following. It never starts following someone else on its own.

\textbf{Postcondition:} The call has ended, the robot is stopped and FOLLOW is off. No audio or video was recorded.

\subsection*{UC-01 acceptance criteria}

\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}

\item \textbf{AC-01.1 - Consent before camera (main flow).} \textbf{Given} the UC-01 preconditions, \textbf{When} C calls, \textbf{Then} the resident sees C's name within 2 seconds, and the camera and microphone stay off until the resident accepts.

\item \textbf{AC-01.2 - Following during a call (main flow).} \textbf{Given} an active call with FOLLOW on, \textbf{When} the resident walks 5 m to another room and stops, \textbf{Then} the robot never exceeds 0.2 m/s, stops within 1 m of the resident, and the call stays connected the whole time.

\item \textbf{AC-01.3 - Target lost (X1).} \textbf{Given} the robot is following during a call, \textbf{When} the resident leaves the camera's view or another person crosses between them, \textbf{Then} the robot's travel request drops to zero within 1 second, it settles within 25 mm of where it lost the resident, its speed stays under 5 mm/s for a full second within 10 seconds of the loss, the call stays connected, and the robot doesn't move again until the resident restarts following.

\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{UC-02 - Find out why a call didn't connect}

\textbf{Linked story:} US-03. \textbf{Primary actor:} Remote caregiver. \textbf{Secondary actors:} Resident; the robot.

\textbf{Preconditions:}

\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}

\item Caregiver C has the resident's permission to call.

\item The robot is online and no call is active.

\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Main success flow:}

\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}

\item \textbf{Caregiver:} Calls the resident.

\item \textbf{System:} Shows C that the robot is ringing.

\item \textbf{Resident:} Accepts the call.

\item \textbf{System:} Shows C that the call is connected.

\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Alternate flow A1 - No answer:} At step 3, the resident doesn't respond for 60 seconds. The system stops ringing and shows C "No answer". It doesn't suggest anything about the resident's health.

\textbf{Exception flow X1 - Connection lost:} At any step, no heartbeat arrives from the robot for 5 seconds. The system ends the attempt and shows C "Robot offline" with the time it was last online.

\textbf{Postcondition:} C sees exactly one outcome: Connected, Declined, No answer or Robot offline.

\subsection*{UC-02 acceptance criteria}

\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}

\item \textbf{AC-02.1 - Connected (main flow).} \textbf{Given} the UC-02 preconditions, \textbf{When} the resident accepts, \textbf{Then} C sees Connected within 2 seconds.

\item \textbf{AC-02.2 - Declined (US-02).} \textbf{Given} the robot is ringing, \textbf{When} the resident declines, \textbf{Then} C sees Declined within 2 seconds, and the resident's camera and microphone never turned on.

\item \textbf{AC-02.3 - Robot offline (X1).} \textbf{Given} the robot is ringing or connected, \textbf{When} 5 seconds pass without a heartbeat, \textbf{Then} C sees Robot offline and the time it was last online, and never sees Connected or No answer for that attempt.

\end{enumerate}
